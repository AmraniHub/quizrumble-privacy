QuizRumble — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how QuizRumble ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Display name — the name you enter when you first open the app. This is stored only on your device (using Android DataStore) and is never sent to our servers, because we don't operate any servers. It is included in the text of challenge links you choose to share with friends.

Information collected automatically (via Google AdMob):

QuizRumble displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable, non-permanent device identifier)
  • Device information (model, OS version, general device settings)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

We do not access, store, or see this data ourselves — it is collected and processed directly by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • Your display name is used solely to personalize the app ("Hi, [name]") and to identify you in challenge links you share with friends.
  • Advertising data collected by AdMob is used to show relevant ads and measure ad performance, per Google's advertising policies.

3. Data Storage

All gameplay data (scores, streaks, coins, language preference, display name) is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate a backend server and do not collect or store this data ourselves. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this local data.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (advertising)
Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google may use the Advertising ID and other signals to personalize ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising via your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization) or through the in-app consent form shown to users in applicable regions (EEA, UK, Switzerland) per Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

5. Children's Privacy

QuizRumble is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

6. Your Choices

  • Ad personalization: You can reset or limit your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google Settings at any time.
  • Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland users): On first launch in these regions, you'll see a consent form governed by Google's UMP, letting you choose whether to allow personalized ads.
  • Local data: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
